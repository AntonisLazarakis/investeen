Monthly leaderboard rules

Last updated 18 October 2026

In short: finish a 3-year "Simulate the future" game, enter with a username, and the player with the highest return at the end of each month wins a prize. It's free, you must be 15 or older, and under-18s need a parent's OK. We only use your email to contact you if you win.

1. Who runs it

The leaderboard is run by InvesTeen (@go.investeen), contact investeen.media@gmail.com. It is not sponsored, endorsed or run by Instagram, Meta, Netlify or any stock exchange.

2. Who can enter

  • You must be at least 15 years old.
  • If you are 15–17, a parent or guardian must know you are entering and agree that we may use your email to contact you about a prize. We may ask the parent to confirm before handing over a prize.
  • Entry is free. No purchase is needed and buying something does not improve your chances.

3. How to enter

  1. Start a "Simulate the future" game on goinvesteen.com while online.
  2. Play all 12 quarters (3 simulated years).
  3. On the final screen, enter a username and email, confirm your age group and accept these rules.

You can play as many games as you like. Only your best entered score each month counts, and one person may use only one email address.

4. How the ranking works

  • Players are ranked by their return after 3 simulated years (final net worth compared with the starting $100,000 of practice money).
  • Each game's market is random and is generated on our server one quarter at a time, after your trades for that quarter are locked in. Luck plays a part, as in real investing.
  • If two players have the same return, the one who entered first ranks higher.
  • Each month runs from the 1st at 00:00 to the last day at 23:59, Greece time (Europe/Athens).

5. Prizes

  • The prize for each month is announced on @go.investeen before or at the start of that month.
  • We email the winner within 7 days after the month ends. If they don't reply within 14 days, or aren't eligible, the prize goes to the next player on the board.
  • Prizes can't be exchanged for cash unless we say so. For winners under 18, the prize may be given with the help of a parent or guardian.
  • We may announce the winning username (never your email) on our website and Instagram.

6. Fair play

We may remove entries or disqualify players who cheat, try to hack or overload the site, use more than one email, or use offensive usernames. Our decisions about the leaderboard are final.

7. Your data

  • We store your username, email, age group, score and entry time.
  • Your email is never shown publicly, never sold, and only used to run this contest and contact you about a prize.
  • We delete emails within 3 months after the month you entered ends.
  • You can ask us to see or delete your data anytime by emailing investeen.media@gmail.com.

8. Not financial advice

The simulator uses practice money and made-up future events. Nothing on InvesTeen is financial advice, and simulated results don't predict real returns.

We may update these rules. Changes won't affect a month that has already ended.
